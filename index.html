01 / Our Story

A local gym with a long-standing place in Rayerbag.

ARS Fitness Center is the first gym in Rayerbag and is widely known and respected in the area. Mr. Abdur Rahim Sheikh was once a general member of the gym and purchased the entire gym in 2018. To bring greater professionalism and modernity to the center, he later sold his own 50% share. The current ownership includes Mr. Abdur Rahim Sheikh and Mr. Rashed; following Mr. Rashed's untimely passing from a heart attack, his wife Suraiya now owns his share.

03 / Equipment Library

Know the machines. Train with purpose.

ARS has machines for major body parts. The cards below explain what each common station is designed to train. These are reference images, not a claim that the pictured brands/models are installed at ARS. Replace them with your own ARS equipment photos for the final public website.

Chest · Triceps · Front delts

Chest Press

A guided pressing movement that trains the chest while also involving the triceps and front shoulders.

How it works

Adjust the seat, keep your back supported, press the handles forward under control and return without letting the stack slam.

Quadriceps

Leg Extension

An isolation station focused on the quadriceps at the front of the thigh.

How it works

Set the pad around the lower shin, keep the hips against the seat and extend the knees smoothly without jerking.

Shoulders · Triceps

Shoulder Press

Vertical pressing for the deltoids with assistance from the triceps and upper chest.

How it works

Set the seat so handles begin around shoulder level, brace your core and press upward through a comfortable range.

Back · Rear delts · Biceps

Seated Row

A horizontal pulling movement that develops the mid-back and supports overall upper-body strength.

How it works

Keep the chest tall, pull the handles toward the torso and squeeze the shoulder blades without excessive swinging.

Cardio · Conditioning

Treadmill

Cardiovascular training for walking, jogging or running at controlled intensity.

Training use

Use a gradual warm-up, choose a pace you can control and increase duration or intensity progressively.

Free weights · Full body

Weights & Benches

Dumbbells, benches and free-weight stations support presses, rows, curls, squats and many accessory movements.

Training use

Free weights allow a wide range of movement patterns. Beginners should prioritize technique and controlled loading.

07 / Gym Etiquette

Train hard. Stay respectful.

Return equipment

Put dumbbells, plates and accessories back after use.

Keep equipment clean

Wipe benches and shared equipment after training.

Respect other members

Share equipment fairly and avoid unnecessary disturbance.

Train safely

Use machines correctly and ask the trainer when unsure.

Use suitable attire

Wear clean workout clothing and appropriate training footwear.

Keep the floor clear

Store bags and personal items safely away from training paths.
